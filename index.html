Job description
The Scheduling Clerk is responsible for scheduling shifts for all employees using computerized time and attendance and scheduling software in accordance with Pioneer Manor's Corporate Policies.

Main Function: Schedule shifts for all employees using a computerized time and attendance and scheduling software in accordance with the ONA and CUPE Local 148 Collective Bargaining Agreements.

Duties: Telephone employees on a daily basis to fill vacancies. Process approved post schedule requests including statutory holidays, vacation, days in-lieu, leaves of absence and shift exchanges. Must work in a fast-paced environment and be available on-call.
